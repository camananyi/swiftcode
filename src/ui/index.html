ACLS Callout Guide

Sample callouts the rules layer logs instantly. Click any phrase to copy it. Speak one callout at a time with a short pause between phrases.

Medication callouts need an action verb: pushing, giving, gave, administered, on board, going in. Rhythm words are recorded verbatim as spoken, never interpreted. Negated phrases ("hold off on epi", "don't give amiodarone") and past references ("we shocked him yesterday") are deliberately ignored. Anything else real but unclassifiable is kept as a note, nothing is dropped.
